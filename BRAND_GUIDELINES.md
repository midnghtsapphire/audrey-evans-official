# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Position
- Product family: Audrey Evans Official / GlowStarLabs
- Tone: premium, direct, helpful, high-trust

## Visual Direction
- Dark-first UI
- Warm amber/orange accents (avoid blue-light-heavy palettes)
- Glassmorphism surfaces for premium feel

## Accessibility
- Alt text for meaningful visual assets
- No flashing/autoplay content
- Visual-only notification support with clear ARIA labels

## Product Language
- Emphasize outcomes, safety, and measurable impact
- Avoid exaggerated or unverifiable claims
